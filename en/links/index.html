Cool Sites

Some interesting and/or useful websites I’ve found:

  • xeiaso.net: A ton of articles and posts on technology, the internet, and that sort of stuff.
  • vkc.sh: Blog and videos about Linux, Open Source, and related stuff.
  • farah.cl: Interesting articles, in particular the ones about keyboards (and some more in spanish).
  • nadeko.net: A bunch of privacy-related services (invidious, rimgo, breezewiki…), hosted in Chile.
  • kartkrew.org: A very good game, Dr. Robotnik’s Ring Racers. It’s a Sonic-themed racing game using Doom’s engine. It has a bunch of community-made addons, and it’s open source.
  • sharyap.com: Shar’s personal page. Very pretty, with nice sounds and immaculate vibes.
  • temstuff.neocities.org: Temmie’s personal page (artist for Undertale and Deltarune, among other things). Pretty simple, but looks cute.
